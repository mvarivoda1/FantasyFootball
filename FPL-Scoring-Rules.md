# Fantasy Premier League – Points Scoring System

> Source: https://fantasy.premierleague.com/help/rules
> Players earn points based on their real-life performances in the Premier League.

## Scoring Summary

| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Playing up to 60 minutes | 1 |
| Playing 60 minutes or more (excluding stoppage time) | 2 |
| Goal scored by a goalkeeper | 10 |
| Goal scored by a defender | 6 |
| Goal scored by a midfielder | 5 |
| Goal scored by a forward | 4 |
| Assist | 3 |
| Clean sheet by a goalkeeper or defender | 4 |
| Clean sheet by a midfielder | 1 |
| Every 3 shot saves by a goalkeeper | 1 |
| Penalty save | 5 |
| Penalty miss | -2 |
| Every 2 goals conceded by a goalkeeper or defender | -1 |
| Yellow card | -1 |
| Red card | -3 |
| Own goal | -2 |
| Bonus points (best performers in a match) | 1–3 |

## Clean Sheets

- A **clean sheet** is awarded for not conceding a goal while on the pitch, and only counts if the player has played **at least 60 minutes** (excluding stoppage time).
- If a player is substituted before the team concedes, the clean sheet still stands for that player (provided 60+ minutes were played).
- **Goalkeepers** and **defenders**: 4 points.
- **Midfielders**: 1 point.
- **Forwards**: no clean sheet points.

## Goals Conceded

- Goalkeepers and defenders lose **1 point for every 2 goals** their team concedes while they are on the pitch.

## Saves

- Goalkeepers earn **1 point for every 3 saves** they make in a match.

## Assists

- An assist is awarded to the player from the team scoring a goal who makes the **final touch** (pass, shot, or any other intentional contribution) before a teammate scores.
- An assist is worth **3 points** for all positions.

## Captaincy

- The **captain's** points are **doubled** (×2).
- If the captain does not play in the gameweek, the **vice-captain** becomes the captain and their points are doubled instead.

## Final Notes

- A player's total points are the sum of all the actions above for each gameweek.
